How it works

  1. 1

    You drop a picture.

    Any photo, drawing, screenshot or meme. It never leaves your laptop.

  2. 2

    An AI reads the depth.

    A depth model works out how far away every single pixel is.

  3. 3

    The pixels move.

    Close stuff pushes forward, far stuff falls back, and your subject climbs out of the panel.

  4. 4

    We open a case file.

    Who's in it, the colors, the vibe, plus covers and stickers to share.
